# Home and about

These pages are public. You do not need an account.

The home page shows the **10 newest published posts**. The about page is a short note about the blog.

## Routes

| Method | Path | Who | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/` | Anyone | Home: latest published posts |
| `GET` | `/about` | Anyone | About Space Blog |

## How it feels

From the navbar you can open **Home**, **Posts**, **About**, and **Login** (or your name once you are signed in). Home also has an **All posts** link to the full list.

If there are no published posts yet, home shows a simple empty message instead of cards.
